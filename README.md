## Explaining the CAD model

### Background

In the process of learning CAD, I felt that I needed a relevant project to test my engineering skills and to work through the process of designing something physically tangible. I eventually came up with the idea of designing a headphone holder for my desk, as my headphones were always sitting on top of and getting in the way of my stack of papers, folders, and books that were on my adjacent table. 

### Functionality of the two parts

Part 1 is the main body which fits into two gaps in my desk snuggly. I took measurements to find the right tolerances. Part 2 is a piece that slides into a slot into the back to keep the first part firmly in place and to stop it from moving around as much as possible
